# 00 Inbox

The capture zone. Anything new — a fleeting thought, a link, a task, a meeting note — lands here first, unsorted.

## Purpose

Inbox exists to lower the friction of capture. Nothing needs to be filed correctly on the way in; it just needs to be written down. Items are triaged later and moved into `01 Projects`, `02 Areas`, `03 Resources`, or `04 Archive` once their category is clear — or discarded if they turn out not to matter.

## Contents

- `daily-notes/` — one note per day, used as a running log and a landing pad for the day's capture

## Workflow

1. Capture first, organize later — don't let filing decisions slow down getting something out of your head.
2. Review the inbox regularly (daily or every few days) and clear it out by moving items to their proper PARA folder.
3. An empty-ish inbox is the goal; a growing backlog means review cadence needs to increase.

# 02 Areas

Ongoing responsibilities with a standard to be maintained over time, but no finish line.

## Purpose

An Area is a sphere of activity you're responsible for indefinitely — health, finances, a role at work, a relationship. Unlike a Project, it isn't "completed"; it's maintained. If a responsibility in an Area spawns a concrete effort with an end date, that effort becomes a Project (and can link back to its Area).

## What belongs here

- Ongoing responsibilities with a standard to uphold
- Roles or spheres of life that persist regardless of any single project
- Notes tracking the active projects and connections relevant to that responsibility

## Workflow

1. New area notes typically start from the `Area Note` template in `../Templates`.
2. Each area note should describe the standard being maintained and link out to related active Projects.
3. Areas generally aren't archived unless the responsibility itself goes away (e.g., a role ends).

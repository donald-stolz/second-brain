# 04 Archive

Inactive items from `01 Projects`, `02 Areas`, and `03 Resources`.

## Purpose

Archive is cold storage, not a trash can. Items land here when they stop being active — a finished or abandoned project, an area you're no longer responsible for, a resource that's no longer relevant — but they're kept in case they're needed again later.

## What belongs here

- Completed or cancelled projects
- Areas of responsibility that have ended
- Resources that are no longer relevant but not worth deleting

## Workflow

1. Move items here as-is when they go inactive; no need to rewrite or clean them up first.
2. If an archived item becomes relevant again, move it back to the appropriate PARA folder rather than duplicating it.
